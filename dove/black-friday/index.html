FROM “LET’S TRY IT” TO “LET’S GO”

Your places.
A plan for everyone.

  1. Save the places you love.

    Explore and save in Dove, or open a place in Apple Maps or Google Maps and use Share to send it to Dove. Keep your ideas organized with lists and tags.

  2. Make a shortlist. Invite friends.

    Use Dove Plus to create a shared Plan from your saved places. Invite friends through iCloud so everyone can weigh in on the same shortlist.

  3. Vote, pick, and go.

    Friends vote Yes, Maybe, or No. The organizer reviews the responses and picks the final destination. Invitees join and vote free.

Free to save. Plus to plan.

Dove is free to download, save, and explore. Dove Plus is required to create Plans. Your invitees don’t need a subscription to join and vote.
