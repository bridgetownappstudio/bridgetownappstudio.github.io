FROM AN IDEA TO AN OUTING

Save it for later.
Make later happen.

  1. 01 / YOUR PLACES

    A home for “let’s try it.”

    Explore the map and save places you’d like to visit. Organize them in lists with tags, or import a place from Apple Maps or Google Maps through the Share sheet.

  2. 02 / YOUR PEOPLE

    A shortlist you can share.

    Use Dove Plus to create a Plan from saved places. Invite people through iCloud so everyone can consider the options together.

  3. 03 / YOUR NEXT OUTING

    A little help choosing.

    Participants vote Yes, Maybe, or No. The organizer reviews the responses and chooses the final destination. Invited participants don’t need a subscription.

A FEW USEFUL DETAILS

Before you go.

At home on your Apple devices.
Available for iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro. Requires iOS 26, iPadOS 26, macOS 26 (Apple M1 or later), or visionOS 26 or later. See the App Store listing for device compatibility.
Choose what you share.
iCloud supports syncing saved-place and planning content across iPhone and iPad. Shared content is available to the people you invite through Apple’s sharing controls. Location access is optional. Read the privacy policy.
