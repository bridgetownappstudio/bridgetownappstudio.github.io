1. Acceptance and eligibility

Use Dove only if you accept Apple’s Standard EULA and can use the app lawfully. Follow the age, account, and permission requirements that apply to the Apple services you use. If you need a parent or guardian’s permission under applicable law, obtain it before using Dove or making a purchase.

2. What Dove does

Dove helps you discover and save places, organize them with tags and lists, and create plans where invited participants can vote and rank choices. Some features depend on your device, permissions, network connection, and Apple’s iCloud and CloudKit services.

Place details can change. Check hours, prices, availability, directions, and other important information with the venue or relevant provider before relying on them.
