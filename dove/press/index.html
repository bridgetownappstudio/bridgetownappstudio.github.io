THE IDEA

Finding a place is one thing.
Choosing it is another.

A promising restaurant. A bar someone recommended. A spot you keep meaning to try. The ideas accumulate, but when it’s time to meet, the same question comes back.

Dove connects saving a place with making a decision. Keep the ideas in collections, bring a few into a shared Plan, and give the people you’re going with a way to weigh in.

02

FROM IDEA TO OUTING

Keep it. Shortlist it.
Pick one together.

  1. 01

    Keep the places

    Save places and organize them in collections, ready for the next occasion.

  2. 02

    Make the Plan

    Bring a shortlist together and invite the people you’re meeting.

  3. 03

    Choose and go

    Participants vote Yes, Maybe, or No. The organizer reviews responses and chooses the final place.

Creating a Plan requires Dove Plus. Invited participants can join and vote without their own subscription. See current pricing and subscription terms on the App Store.
