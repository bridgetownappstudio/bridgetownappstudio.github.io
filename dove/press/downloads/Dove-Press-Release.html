Dove turns “Where should we go?” into a shared decision

Save the places you want to try. Make a shortlist. Choose together.

PORTLAND, Ore., November 8, 2026 — Dove helps people turn the places they want to try into plans they can make together. Available for iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro, Dove brings saved places, personal collections, and shared decisions into one app.

People can save places for later, organize them into collections, and create a shared Plan when it is time to choose. Friends vote Yes, Maybe, or No, and the organizer picks the destination.

Saving and exploring places are free. Dove Plus is required to create a Plan. Invitees can join and vote without their own subscription. Current local pricing, trial eligibility, and renewal terms are shown in the App Store and before purchase.

Dove, pronounced “DOH-veh,” takes its name from the Italian question “where to?” It is made by Karan, the independent developer behind Bridgetown App Studio in Portland, Oregon.

Intelligent features. Privacy built in.

No separate email signup is required. Dove uses iCloud and CloudKit for personal data and chosen sharing. Plan and list content is shared with the people you invite through Apple’s sharing controls.

Apple Intelligence features depend on compatible devices, settings, language, and region. Supported workflows can use on-device processing and Private Cloud Compute. Limited product analytics use Mixpanel; Apple and RevenueCat handle purchases and subscription access.

Availability

Dove launched on November 8, 2026. It requires iOS 26, iPadOS 26, macOS 26 (Apple M1 or later), or visionOS 26 or later.
