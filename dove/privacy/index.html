Privacy at Dove

Privacy Policy

A plain-language explanation of the information Dove handles, where it goes, and the choices available to you.

Effective: August 5, 2026 Last updated: August 5, 2026

The short version

  • Dove stores your saved places, tags, profile name, and plans using on-device storage and Apple’s iCloud and CloudKit services.
  • Your private CloudKit data is private to your iCloud account by default. Plan or list content is shared only when you choose people through Apple’s sharing controls.
  • Location is optional and used for current-location and nearby-map features. Dove does not send coordinates to Mixpanel.
  • Analytics-enabled versions may send limited, anonymous product events to Mixpanel. Dove does not use advertising IDs, persistent identity, People profiles, IP geolocation, or cross-app tracking.
  • We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

1. Scope

This policy applies to Dove, including its Apple-platform app and Dove’s Share extension. Dove helps you find and save places, organize them with tags, import places shared from Apple Maps, and create plans that invited participants can vote on and rank.

This policy does not control Apple, Mixpanel, restaurant websites, or other third-party services you choose to open. Their own policies apply to their services.

2. Data Dove handles

Dove handles the following information when you use the related feature:

  • Saved places and tags Place and tag names; addresses; coordinates for the place; category; phone number; website, Apple Maps, image, and Instagram links; hours; summaries; and the tags or collections you assign. Dove may also save public menu and event metadata used to enrich a saved place.
  • Plans, votes, rankings, and sharing Plan titles, notes, dates and times, candidate places, selected winners, archive status, participant display names, yes/maybe/no votes, ordered rankings, participant roles, and Apple sharing state.
  • Optional profile information and settings A display name you choose for shared plans, plus local settings such as appearance. Your Dove display name is not your Apple Account name.
  • Location, when authorized Your current device location for nearby results, map centering, and distance-based place discovery. See Location for details.
  • Apple Maps shared-place imports When you share a place to Dove, the Share extension can read the shared MapKit item, Apple Maps URL, or supported text. This can include a place name, address, coordinates, category, phone number, website, Apple Maps identifiers, and time zone.
  • Restaurant website and menu metadata Dove may fetch publicly available restaurant pages, related menu pages, PDFs, and images to find hours, menus, events, summaries, and images. This happens for place-detail and enrichment features.
  • Support communications If you email us, we receive your email address and the message and files you choose to send. Support email is separate from Dove’s in-app and CloudKit data.

3. How Dove uses data

Dove uses this information to:

  • provide place search, maps, saved-place, tag, menu, and planning features;
  • sync supported app data across your Apple devices through iCloud;
  • share a list or plan with the people you choose;
  • show votes, rankings, and plan results to authorized participants;
  • complete Apple Maps imports and continue work from the Share extension;
  • maintain app reliability and respond to support requests; and
  • understand aggregate feature use through the limited analytics described in Anonymous product analytics, when that analytics-enabled version and configuration are in use.

4. iCloud, CloudKit, and Apple services

Private data

Dove syncs saved-place core details, tags and tag membership, and your optional Dove display name through the private database in Dove’s CloudKit container. Plans start in the owner’s private CloudKit database. Apple describes a private CloudKit database as accessible only to its user by default and not visible in the developer portal.

Some enrichment and cache data remains on your device instead of syncing. For example, Dove keeps certain downloaded menu or event details and map search caches in local app storage.

Shared data

Dove uses Apple’s CKShare system for shared lists and plans. Public access is disabled. A recipient can access shared records only after the owner shares them and the recipient accepts through Apple’s sharing flow. Access is then controlled by the share’s participant role and permissions.

Shared plan participants can see the plan content and participant activity needed for collaboration, including display names, places, votes, rankings, and the selected winner. A shared saved list can include the list name and selected place details such as addresses and coordinates.

What Bridgetown App Studio can access

Dove does not operate a separate Bridgetown App Studio server that copies your private CloudKit records. Under the app’s current architecture, we do not browse your private CloudKit database through a developer dashboard. Dove code running for your signed-in iCloud account reads and writes the records needed for the features you use. Apple operates iCloud, CloudKit, MapKit, and Apple Maps under Apple’s privacy policy.

iCloud availability, sync behavior, account permissions, and sharing are also controlled by your Apple Account and device settings.

5. Location

Dove asks for When In Use location permission when a feature needs your location. If you allow it, Dove uses the current coordinate to center the map, find nearby places, and provide distance-based behavior. Dove and MapKit may hold the current coordinate in memory, and Dove may keep it in local map or search caches that support those results.

Your current device location is not stored as part of your Dove profile, is not added to CloudKit as a location history, and is not sent to Mixpanel. Coordinates belonging to a restaurant or other place are different: Dove stores those when you save that place or add it to a plan.

You can deny location permission and still use features that do not need it. You can review or change permission at any time in Apple Settings.

6. Anonymous product analytics

Dove’s analytics implementation is being prepared for a future analytics-enabled app version. It applies only when the Dove build you use includes that implementation and has a valid Mixpanel project configuration. Without a valid configuration, analytics is a no-op and no events are sent to Mixpanel.

What an analytics-enabled version sends

Dove sends only a small set of successful product actions, such as creating a saved place, opening an Explore place detail, creating or archiving a plan, submitting a vote or ranking, selecting a winner, preparing a plan share, or completing a shared-place import. Properties are limited to fixed categories and count ranges, such as the feature source, vote choice, import outcome, or a broad number-of-choices bucket.

The Mixpanel SDK also supplies basic technical context used for aggregate reliability analysis, such as Dove’s app and build version, operating system version, Apple device model, screen dimensions, SDK version, and connection type. Each app launch uses a new random identifier. Dove does not join that identifier to your profile, Apple Account, CloudKit data, or identifiers from another launch.

What Dove deliberately excludes

Analytics does not include:

  • your name, email address, or participant names;
  • search text or place, tag, list, or plan names;
  • addresses, device or place coordinates, or location history;
  • URLs, sharing links, restaurant website content, or menu content;
  • CloudKit record identifiers or Apple Account details;
  • raw errors, stack traces, or other free-form diagnostic text; or
  • the advertising identifier or another cross-app advertising ID.

Automatic event capture, persistent Dove user or device identity, Mixpanel People profiles, aliases, IP-based geolocation, attribution, advertising integrations, feature flags, and session replay are disabled. The Share extension does not link or initialize Mixpanel.

Projects, storage, and retention

The planned configuration uses separate Mixpanel projects for development, TestFlight, and production so test activity is not mixed with production reporting. Dove’s production Mixpanel project is intended to use United States data residency. Mixpanel events are retained for two years and then expire under the project’s retention setting.

Because the random analytics identifier changes every launch and is not tied to your identity, we generally cannot locate all of one person’s analytics events in response to an individual access or deletion request. This separation is intentional and reduces the ability to profile a person across app launches.

Dove’s Mixpanel configuration is designed for first-party product analytics, not the cross-app or cross-site tracking Apple describes for App Tracking Transparency (ATT). Dove does not request ATT permission for this analytics. This paragraph describes Dove’s technical configuration; it is not a legal determination or guarantee about every jurisdiction.

7. Service providers and outside sites

  • Apple Provides the operating system, iCloud, CloudKit, MapKit, Apple Maps, Apple’s share flow, and related account and permission controls. Apple processes data needed to provide those services.
  • Mixpanel Processes the limited events described above on our behalf only when an analytics-enabled Dove version and valid configuration are used. Read Mixpanel’s privacy information.
  • Restaurant and related websites When Dove opens or fetches a public webpage, menu, PDF, or image, that site and its hosting providers receive a network request. As with a normal web request, they may receive the requesting IP address, Dove’s user-agent string, the requested URL, and other standard connection information under their own policies.
  • Email provider Our email provider processes messages you send to the privacy or support address so that we can receive and respond to them.

8. Sharing and disclosures

Dove shares plan or saved-list content only when you use Apple’s sharing controls. The owner chooses or approves participants, and CloudKit enforces the share permissions. Dove does not make these shares public. Participants may still copy or redistribute information they can see, so invite only people you trust.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use Dove information for targeted advertising, and we do not share it with data brokers. We may disclose information when you direct us to, when needed for the service providers described in this policy, or when required to comply with a valid legal obligation or protect the security and rights of users and the service.

9. Retention, deletion, and your controls

  • Saved places and tags: Dove provides controls to remove saved places and delete tags or lists. CloudKit propagates supported synced-data changes across your devices.
  • Sharing: A saved-list owner can stop sharing. Apple’s share controls govern participant access. A participant may retain copies they made outside Dove.
  • Plans: Dove currently provides an archive control rather than complete plan deletion. Archiving hides a plan from active use but does not erase its CloudKit records.
  • Profile: You can edit your Dove display name. The current app does not provide a separate delete-profile button.
  • Location: You can deny or revoke location permission in Apple Settings.
  • Mixpanel: Analytics events are retained for two years. The per-launch identifiers are deliberately not tied to you, which generally prevents us from finding every event from one person for individual deletion.
  • Support email: You can ask us to delete support correspondence associated with your email address, subject to records we need to retain for security or legal obligations.

Removing Dove from a device does not necessarily delete records stored in iCloud. Your Apple Account and iCloud settings also affect storage and access. Because private CloudKit records are not visible to us through the developer portal, we may not be able to locate or delete those records for you from our side. Contact us if you need help understanding the controls available for your situation.

10. Security

Dove relies on Apple’s platform protections for app storage, permissions, iCloud, and CloudKit sharing. Network requests use the security available from Apple’s networking stack and the destination service. We limit analytics fields by design and separate analytics from Dove profile and CloudKit identifiers. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure, so we continue to review the app’s data flows and dependencies as Dove changes.

11. International processing

Apple determines the locations and systems used for Apple services under its policies. If Mixpanel analytics is enabled, Dove’s Mixpanel event data is stored and processed in the United States. If you use Dove outside the United States, this means those limited analytics events may be transferred to and processed in the United States.

12. Policy changes and contact

We may update this policy when Dove’s features, service providers, or data practices change. We will update the date at the top of this page. If a change materially affects how Dove handles information, we will provide additional notice when appropriate.
