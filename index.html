THE THINKING BEHIND THE APP

Apple technologies.
A focused purpose.

Familiar interfaces, connected places, and thoughtful use of the platform — all in service of the next “where to?”

01 / SWIFTUI + MAPKIT

Native from the start.

Dove is built with SwiftUI, with MapKit at the heart of exploring places. The interface follows the Apple device you’re using.

02 / ICLOUD + CLOUDKIT

Your places, connected.

iCloud and CloudKit support saved-place data and chosen sharing. Plans begin in the owner’s private database; invited participants access shared content through Apple’s sharing controls.

How Dove uses iCloud →
03 / APPLE INTELLIGENCE

Enhanced details, when available.

Supported place-detail workflows can use Apple Intelligence. Availability depends on compatible devices, settings, language, and region.

For the full picture of data handling, including limited product analytics and subscription services, read the Dove privacy policy.
